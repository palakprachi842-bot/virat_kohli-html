VIRAT KOHLI

INTRODUCTION

Virat Kohli is a famous Indian cricketer and one of the most successful batsmen in international cricket. He is a right-handed batsman who has represented India in all three formats of cricket. He is known for his passion, discipline, hard work, and aggressive style of play. He has also served as the captain of the Indian cricket team.

Early Life

Virat Kohli was born on 5 November 1988 in Delhi, India. He developed an interest in cricket at a very young age. His father, Prem Kohli, was a lawyer, and his mother, Saroj Kohli, was a homemaker. Virat started playing cricket seriously when he was a child. At the age of nine, he joined the West Delhi Cricket Academy, where he trained under coach Rajkumar Sharma. His dedication and hard work helped him develop his skills and become a successful young cricketer.
